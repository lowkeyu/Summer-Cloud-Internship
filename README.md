# Cloud Operation Lab: Summer Volunteer Internship

**Institution:** Illinois Institute of Technology (IIT)  
**Supervisor:** Professor Hajek  

---

## Executive Summary
During my time as a Cloud Operation Lab Volunteer Intern under Professor Hajek, I gained hands-on experience in hardware diagnostics, enterprise server maintenance, and data center networking. The primary mission of the internship was to evaluate, troubleshoot, and salvage donated enterprise servers of unknown operational status, transforming potential e-waste into functional infrastructure for the university.

---

## Key Projects & Responsibilities

### 1. Hardware Diagnostics & Server Salvaging
IIT regularly receives hardware donations from various supporters, but many arrive with unknown operational statuses. 
* **Troubleshooting Process:** I systematically took apart non-functional servers to diagnose issues, repair components, and prevent unnecessary disposal. 
* **Thermal Management Case Study:** For a particularly stubborn unit that booted for only a few seconds before shutting down due to overheating, I:
  * Reapplied thermal paste.
  * Inspected and verified all fan connections and case integrity.
  * Investigated heatsink placement and airflow dynamics.
  * *Result:* Successfully extended the server's uptime from 3 seconds to approximately 20 seconds while continuing advanced diagnostic isolation.
* **Hardware Inventory & Auditing:** For every successfully salvaged server, I used a custom Linux portable USB bootable utility (**Prometheus**) to capture essential hardware metrics:
  * CPU architecture and core count
  * Clock speed ($GHz$)
  * RAM capacity
  * RAID controller identification and model

### 3. Custom Server Assembly & Rack Integration
As part of an initiative to build a fully functional server from an empty rack case:
* **Chassis Preparation:** Installed sliding rails, solved hardware compatibility challenges regarding mismatched screw types and threading, and populated the empty chassis completely.
* **Troubleshooting Final Units:** Assigned the critical task of troubleshooting the final three top servers in the newly built rack, successfully restoring one to full operation.
